## About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in the Interdisciplinary Program in Bioinformatics at Seoul National University, working in the [Biomedical Intelligence Lab (BMI Lab)](https://sites.google.com/view/snuh-bmi-lab/home) at Seoul National University Hospital. I have a background in Computer Science and prior research experience in artificial intelligence, including test-time preference alignment of large language models ([PALC](https://openreview.net/forum?id=0cmuYj3WeG), ICLR 2026).

## Research Interests

My research focuses on bioinformatics and biological foundation models for DNA, RNA, protein, and multi-omics data. I am especially interested in developing graph-based foundation models that explicitly represent the relational structure of biological systems, from molecular interactions to cellular neighborhoods and tissue-level organization. In particular, I aim to design scalable architectures for spatial multi-omics that jointly model molecular measurements, spatial context, and cross-cellular communication. Through this work, I seek to build generalizable and interpretable models that can uncover biological mechanisms, improve disease understanding, and contribute to precision medicine.
